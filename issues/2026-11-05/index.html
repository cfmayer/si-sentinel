The Morning Picture

Agents place the call and shop for you, Starlink Mobile picks Qualcomm for a nationwide hybrid network, and AI starts rewriting and patching software at scale

Elon Musk spent the past day pitching Grok Bot as a phone-and-shopping assistant that can call a dentist, handle an insurance renewal mid-call, and order and return purchases. Cursor's Michael Truell amplified the phone feature, while OpenAI promoted GPT-Live outbound telephony to general availability. SpaceX's Starlink Mobile said it is building the first fully integrated US nationwide satellite-and-terrestrial network, with Qualcomm Dragonwing chips for 5G base stations starting in 2027, and Musk pointed early Starlink customers to free Standard kit upgrades.

David Heinemeier Hansson shipped Omarchy 4.5 with M1/M2/M3 and MacBook Neo support, a traditional desktop mode, 50 languages and seven-second installs. Guillermo Rauch said AI will rewrite slow, vulnerable software, and posted first-day Vercel Security Review numbers; Greg Brockman said OpenAI's cyber pipeline found and patched nearly a thousand Linux local privilege escalations. Alexandr Wang quoted App Store download figures putting Muse ahead of ChatGPT in the US last week, and Conductor said it is joining Meta's Superintelligence Labs.

I · Agents on the Phone

Grok Bot places the call and shops; OpenAI opens outbound telephony

Musk pitched phone calls, mid-call question cards and shopping returns. Cursor's Michael Truell echoed the appointment use case, and OpenAI made GPT-Live outbound calling generally available.

6+Musk posts overnight and through the day promoting Grok Bot calls, shopping and assistants
GAGPT-Live outbound telephony status, via any Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) trunk provider
DTMFKeypad tones Live agents can send and receive on those calls

Elon Musk @elonmusk · CEO, SpaceX and Tesla; xAI

Musk quoted a user whose Grok Bot called car insurance about a 14% renewal hike with no accidents and got a mid-call question card instead of hanging up. At 12:44pm EST he wrote:

"Try Grok @Bot. It's like hiring an amazing assistant!"
Elon Musk · @elonmusk · 12:44pm EST · post

At 10:00am EST he sketched the shopping loop: "Grok @Bot will research purchase ideas, bring back a selection, order them, track the purchases and return anything you don't want." Overnight he added "Grok @Bot can call your dentist on the phone!" at 2:59am EST.

Michael Truell @mntruell · CEO, Cursor

Truell quoted Grok Bot's phone-call launch at 1:53pm EST: "Ask Bot to schedule appointments, cancel subscriptions, or coordinate with colleagues over the phone."

Greg Brockman · GPT-Live telephony @gdb · President, OpenAI

OpenAI's Justin Uberti said the company had promoted its GPT-Live outbound telephony application programming interface (API) to general availability, so developers can place calls from a Live voice agent through any SIP trunk provider such as Telnyx or Twilio, with high-definition voice and Dual-Tone Multi-Frequency (DTMF) keypad events. Brockman pointed to the launch at 11:52pm EST: "GPT-Live for outbound telephony."

II · Starlink Mobile

Starlink Mobile picks Qualcomm Dragonwing for a nationwide hybrid network

SpaceX framed Starlink Mobile as the first fully integrated US nationwide satellite-and-terrestrial phone network. Qualcomm will supply Dragonwing cellular infrastructure silicon, with terrestrial 5G rollout aimed at 2027, and early first-generation Starlink customers were offered free Standard kit upgrades.

2027Year Starlink said terrestrial 5G base stations using Qualcomm chips begin deploying
64T64RMassive multiple-input multiple-output radio configuration in Qualcomm's Dragonwing platforms
FreeStandard kit upgrades Musk highlighted for early first-generation Starlink customers

Starlink Mobile and Qualcomm @Starlink · @Qualcomm

Starlink posted at 9:04am EST that "Starlink Mobile is building the first fully-integrated US nationwide satellite and terrestrial network that will keep people connected on their phones no matter where they are," with 5G base stations leveraging Qualcomm chips starting in 2027. Qualcomm CEO Cristiano Amon wrote that SpaceX selected Qualcomm Dragonwing to accelerate Starlink Mobile's nationwide terrestrial 5G network and a path to "AI-native 6G." Qualcomm's press release, also carried on Business Wire, says the multi-year deal uses Dragonwing QRU100 and QDU100 platforms with 64 transmit and 64 receive (64T64R) Massive MIMO radios, with US nationwide deployment beginning in 2027 and tens of thousands of sites planned. Musk quoted Starlink at 10:04am EST:

"At last there will be one network with good signal everywhere!"
Elon Musk · @elonmusk · 10:04am EST · post

He separately wrote "Qualcomm's technology is excellent" at 9:36am EST.

Free dishes for early customers @elonmusk

Musk wrote "Free new dish for all early Starlink customers" at 10:02am EST, quoting a report that eligible customers still on a first-generation kit can receive a new Standard kit at no cost, with a free mount and optional free installation, invited by email. Starlink's support article carries the same eligibility language.

III · Omarchy 4.5

Omarchy 4.5 liberates Apple Silicon Macs

David Heinemeier Hansson shipped Omarchy 4.5 with native installs on M1, M2, M3 and MacBook Neo machines, a traditional desktop mode beside tiling, 50 languages and installs as fast as seven seconds. AMD collaboration and a 4.6 roadmap for Snapdragon, NVIDIA N1x and local AI followed in the same breath.

4.5Omarchy release with Apple Silicon support, floating desktop mode and 50 languages
7 secFull install time Heinemeier Hansson cited from an AMD laptop record
4.6Next release: Snapdragon lineup, NVIDIA N1x polish, out-of-the-box local AI

David Heinemeier Hansson @dhh · Creator, Ruby on Rails and Omarchy

Hours before the final drop, at 9:48am EST, he previewed Apple Silicon support:

"Omarchy 4.5 will ship with full compatibility for M1, M2, M3, and Neo! Incredible achievement from the Omarchy M team. The installer makes this so smooth, the dual boot makes it a no brainer. Apple hardware liberated. M4-6 on the way. WE CAN FIX EVERYTHING!"
David Heinemeier Hansson · @dhh · 9:48am EST · post

When Omarchy's account announced 4.5 — traditional desktop mode, M1/M2/M3/Neo compatibility, 50 languages, seven-second installs — he quoted it at 12:43am EST and said the release was "enormously, awesomely, fantastically better." The release notes credit more than 740 pull requests from more than 180 contributors in twelve weeks, and an AMD laptop install in seven seconds. He had earlier amplified a Phoronix note that "@AMD Loves @omarchy." A minute after the 4.5 quote he previewed 4.6: the full Snapdragon lineup, polished NVIDIA N1x machines, out-of-the-box local AI, and Omarchy Kids.

IV · AI That Patches

Rauch says AI will rewrite software; OpenAI and Vercel show the patching numbers

Guillermo Rauch argued that most human-made software was slow, vulnerable and under-specified, and that AI will rewrite it. Vercel's first-day Security Review stats and OpenAI's Linux privilege-escalation pipeline put concrete counts behind the claim; Suhail Doshi called 24/7 self-pwn scanning table stakes.

6,817Vulnerabilities Vercel Security Review identified in 24 hours, per Rauch
948Linux local privilege escalations OpenAI said one cyber-model run validated and patched
~$1,000API cost per vulnerability Brockman cited for OpenAI's pipeline

Guillermo Rauch @rauchg · CEO, Vercel

At 9:22pm EST Rauch posted:

"We will all be learning in coming weeks & months, that most human-made software: Was really slow. Had many security vulnerabilities. Was under-specified and incorrect. AI will rewrite it all. Many times. It will make it fast, cost-efficient, and secure. … In many ways, we are witnessing the true birth of software engineering."
Guillermo Rauch · @rauchg · 9:22pm EST · post

At 8:23pm EST he posted first-day Vercel Security Review counts: "In just 24 hours: 6,817 vulnerabilities identified; 580 critical or high; 1,411 already patched by Agent. Vercel Security Reviews are 100% free until Nov 10." Vercel's blog and changelog describe the public beta for Pro and Enterprise teams, with reviews on every push and Agent able to open a fix.

Greg Brockman · OpenAI cyber pipeline @gdb

OpenAI said its security researchers built an autonomous cyber vulnerability discovery, exploitation and patching pipeline that, in a single run with an upcoming cyber model, validated and generated patches for 948 Linux kernel local privilege escalations at a total API cost of $1.2 million. Brockman summarized at 6:53pm EST:

"we've found, validated, and prepared patches for almost 1,000 linux local privilege escalations using our cyber vulnerability pipeline, at the cost about $1,000 per vulnerability. an emerging scalable approach to significantly hardening both open source and proprietary projects"
Greg Brockman · @gdb · 6:53pm EST · post

Suhail Doshi @Suhail · CEO, Playground

Doshi quoted a Node.js high-severity advisory at 12:12pm EST: "It is now table stakes to be running a 24/7 process of scanning for vulnerabilities and attempting to self-pwn your software/infra. This will be a compliance requirement for any business doing business with others." He later said he wants to talk to people building security agents.

V · Muse and Conductor

Wang quotes Muse topping US App Store downloads; Conductor joins Meta

Alexandr Wang amplified a third-party App Store tally that put Muse ahead of ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok in US downloads last week. Conductor's Charlie Holtz said the company is joining Meta to work with Nat Friedman and Wang at Meta Superintelligence Labs; Rauch congratulated the move.

942KMuse US App Store downloads last week in the tally Wang quoted
556KChatGPT downloads in the same quoted tally; Gemini 341K, Grok 239K
MSLMeta Superintelligence Labs, where Conductor said it will work with Friedman and Wang

Alexandr Wang @alexandr_wang · Chief AI officer, Meta

Wang quote-posted at 12:57pm EST a chart from Pablo Marti claiming Muse from Meta led US App Store downloads last week at 942,000, against ChatGPT at 556,000, Gemini at 341,000 and Grok at 239,000 — about 1.7 times ChatGPT, eight weeks after launch, with Muse also said to be No. 1 in Productivity in Japan. The figures are from that quoted post, not a Meta press release. Wang's post linked the chart without adding a separate claim of his own.

Conductor joins Meta @charlieholtz · @rauchg · @alexandr_wang

Charlie Holtz, Conductor's founder, announced at 1:18pm EST that Conductor is joining Meta, with "big, crazy plans for the next interface," to work with Nat Friedman and Alexandr Wang at Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL). Rauch replied at 3:18pm EST: "Congratulations. Extremely highly deserved. It's been amazing to support your journey, and can't wait to continue to work with you and the broader Meta team." Wang wrote at 9:38pm EST, "welcome conductor team to meta / muse gang growing."

VI · Open Weights

Ripple-65B Preview claims US open-weight coding SOTA; Together Compiler keeps inference moving

Albert Gu highlighted Ricursive AI's Ripple-65B Preview as a new US open-weight state of the art on agentic coding; Jeff Dean congratulated the team. Simran Arora's Together Compiler post, which Gu amplified, said 80% of OpenRouter traffic already goes to models released in the last three months.

65BParameter count of Ripple Preview, a dense model Ricursive AI said it pretrained from scratch
80%Share of OpenRouter traffic to models released in the last three months, per the Together Compiler post
75.56ARC-AGI-3 score for Yi-Chia Chen, whom François Chollet congratulated

Albert Gu and Jeff Dean @_albertgu · @JeffDean

Ricursive AI released Ripple-65B Preview, a 65-billion-parameter dense large language model pretrained from scratch that it said sets a new state of the art for US open-weight models on agentic coding tasks, ahead of larger models including Muse Spark Open-816B, Beam-501B, Inkling-975B and Nemotron. Gu called it a "new open weights model from a dark horse" at 1:44pm EST. Jeff Dean congratulated Anna Goldie, Azalia Mirhoseini and the Ricursive AI team at 2:07pm EST.

Together Compiler @_albertgu · @simran_s_arora

Gu also quoted Simran Arora of Together AI at 12:56pm EST on the Together Compiler, continual compilation for polymorphic inference. Arora wrote that on OpenRouter, 80% of traffic goes to models released in the last three months, and that the top ten most popular open models each use a different architecture — so deployments must be recompiled as traffic shifts. Gu's gloss: "in a constantly changing model landscape, faster adaptation is the biggest advantage."
